    About these models
    Applied when you save, after your Develop settings, as the desktop app's restoration stage does. Downloaded once from Hugging Face (SceneWorks/real-esrgan-onnx, BSD-3-Clause; svjack/AI-onnx) and kept by the browser, unless they are in your models folder (e.g. models/sr/real_esrgan_x4.onnx).
    When the output is smaller than the model's scale (e.g. original size with a ×4 model), the model always runs at its full ×4 and the result is scaled down afterwards (×¼ for original size), keeping the model's cleaner detail at a normal file size.

    Process

    Quick preview processes a centre crop (about 1500 × 1000 pixels) to try settings fast. Then fine-tune in the Develop panel on the right.
    Re-blend saved column TIFFs
    Change the mix without processing again. Use the column TIFFs made in this session (tick “Also make 16-bit TIFFs of each column” before processing), or open ones you saved earlier (…_col1.tif, …_col2.tif). The blend goes to the Develop panel; for the same photo your Develop settings are kept. Tip: the column TIFFs use the Export colour space; with ProPhoto RGB they keep every colour, so a re-blend matches a fresh one exactly (Adobe RGB / sRGB clip a few very saturated colours).
    Processing log

    About

    Everything runs on this computer: the RAW is decoded with LibRaw (WebAssembly), Bayer files are demosaiced with ports of RawTherapee's AMaZE and RCD (or Malvar–He–Cutler / bilinear), and X-Trans files with LibRaw's Markesteijn 1- or 3-pass. RAW NIND-Unet (joint demosaic + denoise) and RawForge run through ONNX Runtime Web, on the GPU when WebGPU is available.
    The desktop app's BM3D and RawTherapee finishing are not in the browser version.
